# Cast & Canoe — Short-Form Video Scripts

**Status: PRIVATE PREP — concepts and scripts only. Nothing filmed or posted. Canoe films/uploads; Brooke scripts and captions.**
Platforms: TikTok (primary), cross-post natively to IG Reels and YouTube Shorts.
Format rules: vertical 9:16, 20–40s, hook in the first 2 seconds, on-screen text throughout (muted viewers), game audio up, end card = "Cast & Canoe — free in your browser · castandcanoe.com".

---

## 1. "A pet at every lake"
- **Hook (0–2s):** "This fishing game has a pet at EVERY lake."
- **Shots:** Fast cuts, one pet each, name card per pet: Beckham & Olive (Kettle Pond) → Shale the cat (Reynolds dock) → Port and Starboard ducks following in a line (West Hawk) → Juniper the burro (Slickrock) → Boudin the pelican catching a tossed fish (bayou).
- **On-screen text:** "a pet at every lake 🐾" / per-pet names / "feeding each one earns a badge" / end card.
- **Audio:** Game audio; ducks clip gets its own beat — let the follow-in-a-line moment run long.
- **Caption:** "Two dogs, a dock cat, ducks that follow you in a line, a burro, and a pelican. Every lake, one pet. Cast & Canoe — free in your browser 🎣 #cozygames #fishinggame #pixelart"

## 2. "Book a private lake for your friends"
- **Hook:** "You can book a whole lake for just you and your friends."
- **Shots:** Camps sign → booking screen, code appears (PINE-4K7Q style) → friend joining → 3–4 anglers fishing side by side → wave emotes.
- **On-screen text:** "book a camp" / "send your friends the code" / "the lake is yours" / "free · in your browser" / end card.
- **Caption:** "No strangers, no download, just send the code. Cast & Canoe camps — free at castandcanoe.com #cozygames #multiplayer #browsergame"

## 3. "The fish rise at :00"
- **Hook:** "At the top of every hour, the fish in this game RISE."
- **Shots:** Clock/on-screen countdown to the hour → Kettle Pond surface coming alive → casts landing → a catch card reveal.
- **On-screen text:** "ten minutes, every hour" / "Kettle Pond, :00 sharp" / end card.
- **Production note:** Must be filmed on the hour — schedule the capture.
- **Caption:** "Fishing o'clock is real. Ten minutes from the top of every hour at Kettle Pond. Set a kettle on. ☕🎣 #fishinggame #cozygaming"

## 4. "Wave hello"
- **Hook:** "Saying hi in this fishing game looks like this."
- **Shots:** Angler arrives at a busy dock → wave emote with hand beside head → the other angler waves back → dance emote → chat bubble with name in shirt colour floating up.
- **On-screen text:** "wave hello 👋" / "names wear your shirt's colour" / "yes, there's dancing" / end card.
- **Caption:** "New: the Wave and the Dance. Because a fishing game should let you say hi properly. Cast & Canoe, free in your browser #cozygames #pixelart #indiegame"

## 5. "Your catch, pinned up properly"
- **Hook:** "This is the most satisfying catch screen in any fishing game."
- **Shots:** Cast → bobber → bite (!) → reel fight → catch card pins the fish up on its water, ribbons appearing → flip to the aquarium where it's kept.
- **On-screen text:** "every catch is one of a kind" / "ribbons for its awards" / "keep it in your aquarium" / end card.
- **Caption:** "Catch cards in Cast & Canoe. The fish are real fish from real lakes — the ribbons are earned. 🎣 #fishinggame #cozygames #gamedev"

## 6. "The developer is IN the game"
- **Hook:** "The guy who made this game? You can meet him in it."
- **Shots:** Andre at Kettle Pond station → Andre's list opening → Brooke fishing by the Launch, cheer her catch → departure board showing where the guides are → wave at Andre.
- **On-screen text:** "Andre is played only by the game's maker" / "Brooke, the other guide, minds the station" / "say hi — it's really him" / end card.
- **Caption:** "Handmade game, maker included. Cast & Canoe — free in your browser, no download. castandcanoe.com #indiedev #cozygames #gamedev"
