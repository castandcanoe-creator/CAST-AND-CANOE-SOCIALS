## Voice

- Warm, plain, a little playful — the game's own version notes are the model ("wave hello", "a pet at each lake"). Short sentences. Stories over specs.
- Write as the game/team ("we"), sign personal notes from Andre or Brooke when it's genuinely one of them.
- Every post makes *one* point and, where natural, ends at castandcanoe.com.
- Never oversell: no fake urgency, no engagement bait, no "smash that follow". Cozy audience, cozy tone.

## Channel plan

### Facebook Page — the community noticeboard
- **Role:** longest-form channel; best fit for families and fishing-game fans; event/session announcements ("fishing o'clock" at the top of the hour), update stories, photo albums per lake.
- **Cadence:** 3 posts/week (shared plan with IG — see calendar), plus a pinned "start here" post after launch: what the game is, play link, how-to-play-with-friends summary.
- **Formats:** long update-story posts (patch notes rewritten as stories), screenshot albums, camp-session invitations, community catch round-ups (with permission).
- **Brooke can:** draft, post, and schedule via connector; reply to comments with drafted answers.

### Instagram — the pretty shop window
- **Role:** cozy-gamer discovery; the visual channel. Beauty shots, pet content, carousels.
- **Cadence:** 3 posts/week + Reels cross-posted from the TikTok short-form set (upload natively, don't watermark-share).
- **Formats:** per-lake carousels (same framing, one pet per lake), Reels (vertical clips from the shot list), Stories for session reminders ("fish rise at the top of the hour — see you at Kettle Pond").
- **Brooke can:** draft, post, and schedule via connector once the account is linked; prepare Stories assets. Canoe still needs to add the website link in the app once.

### TikTok — discovery engine, manual upload
- **Role:** where cozy-game clips travel. Pets, satisfying casts, "did you know" mechanics (top-of-the-hour fish rise, camp codes).
- **Cadence:** 2–3 clips/week from launch, from the 6 scripted concepts in `short-form-scripts.md` first.
- **Formats:** 20–40s vertical, hook in first 2 seconds, on-screen text (many viewers are muted), game audio up.
- **Brooke can:** script, write captions/hashtags, prep shot lists, research hooks via vidIQ. **Canoe uploads** (no connector). Keep a shared "ready to upload" folder so upload day is 2 minutes per clip.

### YouTube — the deep end
- **Role:** launch video, devlogs, lake tours; Shorts mirror the TikTok set. Community tab for update stories and session announcements once enabled.
- **Cadence:** 1 long video at launch, then aim 2/month + Shorts as they're made.
- Full plan: `../YOUTUBE/README.md`.
- **Brooke can:** research (vidIQ), scripts, titles/descriptions/tags, thumbnail briefs. **Canoe films, edits, uploads.**
